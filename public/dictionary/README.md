# Offline dictionary

`ecdict.json` is a 20,000-word subset of [ECDICT](https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT), selected by corpus frequency. Each entry is `[word, phonetic, Chinese translation, normalized part of speech, English definition]`. The optional fifth field keeps the original four columns compatible. 19,761 of 20,000 entries (98.805%) have an English definition; missing entries use AI when configured. Definitions retain at most two complete senses and 500 characters. General dictionary senses may not cover specialist meanings; AI supplies the in-context meaning separately.

The data is generated by `scripts/build_dictionary.py` from the ECDICT CSV. ECDICT and this generated subset are distributed under the MIT license; see `LICENSE`.

ECDICT is an offline English-Chinese dictionary assembled by skywind3000. This project only bundles a small frequency-filtered subset for instant word lookups; AI remains responsible for contextual meaning.
